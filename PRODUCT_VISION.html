SINGULARITY

the vision

Seen it. On it. Fixed it.

Customers don’t experience problems on our timeline. By the time they tell us, the moment of frustration has already passed.

Singularity acts in the moment: seeing it, getting on it, reaching the customer, alerting the team, and driving the fix.

It brings together PostHog, Last9, Linear, and Slack to connect the dots: what the customer experienced, what happened underneath, who needs to act, and what needs to be fixed.

how it comes together

the problem

Every product is instrumented twice, and watched by no one.

PostHog sees what the customer experienced. Last9 sees what happened underneath. The click that didn’t respond. The flow they abandoned. The request that timed out. The queue that backed up.

The problem lives in the gap between those signals. A customer struggles, the system quietly regresses, and nobody connects the two until the customer tells you.

how it comes together

One brain. Two ears. Three ways to act.

Singularity listens to both sides of the product at once, connects the signals, and decides what matters, then acts in three directions: the customer, the team, and the code.

scanning
PostHog live today

Behavior signal: clicks, rage clicks, dead clicks, front‑end errors, and the session context around each one.

Singularity the core

Correlates both streams per session, judges what’s a real bug worth acting on, and decides who needs to know.

Slack next

The team finds out the moment it matters, in the channel they already live in. No dashboard to remember to check.

Last9 next

System signal: logs, traces, latency, and error rates, what the infrastructure actually did underneath them.

Linear next

A ticket gets filed with the evidence already attached: the session, the trace, the exact moment it broke.

Customer the goal

The person it happened to hears from us first, before they have to file a ticket or leave a review.

Pull request future

From Linear, the fix drafts itself: a pull request raised automatically against the exact code the ticket points to, ready for someone to review instead of write from scratch.

why this order matters

Detecting a problem isn’t the hard part. Acting while it matters is.

Singularity turns scattered signals into one judgment, then closes the loop: the customer gets reached while the pain is fresh, the team knows what happened, and the work gets tracked and fixed.

The goal isn’t just to catch problems. It’s to turn a moment of frustration into a moment of trust.

the thesis

We’re not building another dashboard.

We’re building the layer that watches the dashboards for you. It connects what the customer experienced with what the system did, and acts before the customer has to ask.

See it. On it. Before they say anything.
